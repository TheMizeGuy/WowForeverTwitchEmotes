Twitch Emotes WoW Forever 3.2.0 adds a channel group and a first-login channel picker. It runs on WoW Forever 1.60.1, Retail 12.1.5, Mists of Pandaria Classic 5.5.4, Burning Crusade Anniversary 2.5.6, and Classic Era 1.15.9.

**Fully exit and restart WoW after installing this update.** `/reload` alone can leave new emote textures as green boxes until the client restarts.

- One new channel group with 565 emote names, 335 of them animated, brings the collection to 67 groups and about 8,500 names. Like every added channel it starts disabled; tick it under Channels.
- The first time you enter the world after installing, the window opens on the channel picker so you can tick the groups you want. General and five channel groups start ticked, and your existing choices are kept. The picker opens once: Finish or closing the window ends it, and the Channels tab brings it back whenever you like. It waits until you are out of combat.
- The download stays under 200 MB. To make room, 558 of the 3,082 earlier animations are stored at a smaller frame height (most by one step, 105 by two or three); no emote and no frame of timing was removed.

Every Twitch global text face (`:)`, `:-P`, `:/`, `<3`, `O_o`, `B)` and the rest), `D:`, and the chat words `1G`, `BOP`, `Retail`, and `Classic` stay disabled by default and can be restored individually.

Open the browser with `/te`, `/twitch`, or the minimap button.
