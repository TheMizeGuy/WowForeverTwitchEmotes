Twitch Emotes WoW Forever 3.0.0 brings animated chat emotes and a purple visual browser to WoW Forever 1.60.1.

**Fully exit and restart WoW after installing this update.** `/reload` alone can leave new emote textures as green boxes until the client restarts.

- More than 8,000 emote names across 66 groups, with one group per channel.
- Full collection in a download under 200 MB (1.3 GB installed), keeping every emote, every frame of animation, and source timing; static emotes stay at 64 px and animations use the largest frame height the budget allows, chosen per emote.
- General and five channel groups enabled by default, with no automatic setup popup.
- Search and preview every installed group, then enable it directly from Browse.
- Favorites, recent emotes, individual hiding, and controls for shared emote names.
- Autocomplete with emote image previews and an adjustable 12–40 pixel chat size.
- Source-timed animation, visible-line updates, and a compact movable minimap button.

The text faces `:)`, `:(`, `:O`, `:D`, and `D:` are disabled by default and can be restored individually.

Open the browser with `/te`, `/twitch`, or the minimap button. Use Channels or Settings > Choose channels to adjust the preset selection.

Known beta issue: the Forever client may reset addon settings after reloads or restarts. This release uses its preset selection when settings are unavailable. Manual changes work for the current session; automatic first-run setup is postponed until the client saves settings reliably.
