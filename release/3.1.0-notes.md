Twitch Emotes WoW Forever 3.1.0 runs on every current World of Warcraft client: WoW Forever 1.60.1, Retail 12.1.5, Mists of Pandaria Classic 5.5.4, Burning Crusade Anniversary 2.5.6, and Classic Era 1.15.9.

**Fully exit and restart WoW after installing this update.** `/reload` alone can leave new emote textures as green boxes until the client restarts.

- One download serves all five clients. Each client loads its own manifest, and the AddOn List names the game version, for example "Twitch Emotes (Classic Era)".
- Autocomplete now uses the addon's own suggestion list instead of driving Blizzard's chat autocomplete, which tainted the active chat box for the rest of the session. Up and Down move the highlight, and Tab or a click inserts the name while the chat box keeps focus. Enter always sends your message as typed, so `lol :P` goes out unchanged.
- Clicking an emote in the browser, or Shift-clicking one in chat, inserts its name only when the chat box is already open. The addon never opens chat for you.
- The emote browser and the emote menu let movement and ability keys through, in combat too.
- Minimap button collectors such as MinimapButtonButton, Leatrix Plus, and EllesmereUI can now gather the minimap button. When another addon has loaded LibDBIcon, the button takes LibDBIcon's standard look and also appears in broker displays such as Titan Panel.
- Chat matching leaves web addresses, escaped pipes, runs of punctuation, brackets, and non-English punctuation alone, and autocomplete respects the chat box's 255-byte limit.
- Hidden emotes, favorites, and usage counts survive a pack update that drops a name.
- Animation redraws a line only when one of its frames changes, and lines with more than 16 links stay still.
- Retail and Forever gain a Guild Discord channel toggle. A whisper to yourself counts once in usage statistics, and censored lines you reveal now show their emotes.

Every Twitch global text face (`:)`, `:-P`, `:/`, `<3`, `O_o`, `B)` and the rest), `D:`, and the chat words `1G`, `BOP`, `Retail`, and `Classic` stay disabled by default and can be restored individually.

Open the browser with `/te`, `/twitch`, or the minimap button.
